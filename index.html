FAQs

Before you order.

Will the website show our actual wedding card photos?

Yes. Replace the sample placeholders with your own photographs in the assets/cards folder and update the matching image paths in index.html.

Can customers ask about a specific card model?

Yes. Each card listing can show a model code. Customers can click Request quote and the selected card name/code will be included in the WhatsApp message.

Can customers upload a card reference?

The form lets them choose a file locally, but GitHub Pages cannot receive or store that file. They must attach it manually after WhatsApp opens.

Do you sell gifts?

No gift category is included. This site focuses on wedding cards, banners and the printing services listed above.
